# Phase 11 Store Readiness

## App Title

Tomeza: AI Book Maker

## Short Description

Create guided ebooks, workbooks, and short stories from a prompt.

## Long Description

Turn a book idea into a structured ebook, workbook, or short story with a guided mobile workflow.

Tomeza helps creators, teachers, coaches, consultants, and small business users draft practical book projects from a focused prompt. Choose a launch-ready preset, describe the audience and goal, review an outline, request revisions, approve the plan, then generate a book preview and protected PDF/EPUB exports.

What you can do:

- Create lead magnet ebooks, workbooks, study guides, and short stories.
- Review and revise an AI-generated book plan before full generation.
- Generate page previews, covers, and selected visuals.
- Unlock bounded export packages with credits.
- Download and share PDF or EPUB files.
- Report generated books or visuals for safety review.
- Delete projects and request account deletion from account settings.

Important notes:

- Books and visuals are generated with AI from your prompt and selected product preset.
- Tomeza is not positioned as a child-directed app for launch.
- Health, legal, financial, safety, and other sensitive topics are for general educational drafting only and are not professional advice.
- Paid usage is credit-based and bounded. Tomeza does not offer unlimited AI generation.
